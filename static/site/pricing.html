Pricing questions?

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Nothing scary. We WhatsApp you, ask how it went, and you pick a plan. If it's not for you, your data is deleted on request — no auto-charges, ever (we don't even have your card).

What counts as a "member"?

Anyone who logs in — you, managers, employees. Your clients viewing their live dashboard link are NOT members and are always free.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. One WhatsApp message and we upgrade/downgrade you the same day, adjusting what you've already paid.

Is my data safe?

Your workspace is completely isolated from every other company's. Payments & fees are visible to the owner only. Daily-use data is encrypted in transit and stored on managed cloud infrastructure.
